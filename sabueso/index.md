# FamilyPlanner Sabueso

FamilyPlanner Sabueso is a privately operated email-to-planning assistant for personal and household use.

With the account holder's permission, it reads messages and relevant attachments from an authorized Gmail account. Configured source rules select messages for processing. Local extraction tools and locally hosted AI models identify proposed events, which are sent with relevant supporting information to the private FamilyPlanner service.

Its Gmail access is read-only: Sabueso does not send emails or modify or delete messages in Gmail. Calendar changes are handled separately by FamilyPlanner under its own permissions and workflow.

The service is used privately and does not offer public registration.
